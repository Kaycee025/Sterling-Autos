The Sterling Inspection Standard

Our certified master technicians examine every vehicle across four comprehensive technical disciplines before delivery.

Pillar 01

Engine & Transmission

Full cylinder compression tests, fluid analysis, turbocharger spool inspection, transmission shift timing, and cooling system pressure validation under tropical heat conditions.

Pillar 02

Chassis & Suspension

Detailed scrutiny of air suspension bladders, control arm bushings, steering linkages, braking rotors, and ladder-frame chassis alignment on 3D laser alignment benches.

Pillar 03

Electrical & Sensors

Complete ECU diagnostic scans, alternator load testing, radar/camera ADAS calibration, climate control refrigerant efficiency, and soundproofing integrity.

Pillar 04

Provenance & Title

Rigorous verification of customs import documentation, clean Carfax history, non-flood certification, structural accident-free clearance, and authenticated VIN records.
